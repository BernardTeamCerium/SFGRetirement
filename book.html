References to a “pension” or “paycheck” describe a stream of income from an annuity contract and do not refer to an employer-sponsored pension plan.

Upside potential & downside protection. Fixed indexed annuities are insurance contracts. They are not investments in the market and do not directly participate in any stock, bond or equity investment. Index-linked crediting is subject to caps, spreads and participation rates that may change over the life of the contract, so you will not receive the full gain of any index. Protection from market loss applies to principal and credited interest under the terms of the contract and does not protect against surrender charges, rider fees, or withdrawals in excess of contract limits.
