Your retirement paycheck

Create your own pension from the savings you already have

Most people no longer retire with a pension. The good news: with the right strategy, your 401(k), IRA and savings can be structured to do the same job — deliver reliable income every month, for the rest of your life.

Lifetime income

Turn a lump sum into a dependable monthly paycheck designed to arrive on schedule for as long as you live — so you never have to wonder whether the money will run out before you do.

Upside potential

Participate in a portion of market-linked growth so your income and savings have the opportunity to keep pace with inflation and rising costs over a retirement that may last 30 years or more.

Downside protection

Shield the money you can’t afford to lose from market crashes, so a bad year on Wall Street doesn’t become a bad year in your retirement — or force you to sell low to pay the bills.

When markets rise

Your plan is positioned to capture growth, helping your savings and income work harder for you.

When markets fall

Protected assets are designed to hold their ground, so your income keeps coming and your principal stays intact.

What happens in your meeting

A clear conversation, not a sales pitch

Retirement planning is full of conflicting advice, confusing products and fine print. Your meeting with Troy is designed to cut through all of it. Here’s what to expect.

  1. We get to know you and your goals

    Troy starts by listening. What does retirement look like for you? When do you want to retire, what keeps you up at night, and what do you want your money to make possible — for you and the people you love?

  2. We review where you stand today

    Together you’ll look at your savings, 401(k)s, IRAs, pensions and Social Security picture. You’ll see how much risk you’re carrying, what you may be paying in fees, and whether your current approach can actually produce the income you need.

  3. We map your lifetime income

    Troy walks you through how to turn your savings into a pension-like income stream — one that can provide upside potential when markets grow and protection from downside when they don’t — and how it fits with Social Security, taxes and the rest of your plan.

  4. We stress-test it against real-world risks

    Market crashes, inflation, rising taxes, health care costs and living longer than expected. You’ll see how your plan holds up in the years that don’t go according to plan.

  5. You leave with clarity and next steps

    You’ll walk away with plain-English answers and a clear direction. If Sibley Financial Group is a good fit, we’ll outline what a written plan would look like. If not, we’ll tell you that too.
